## Nutrition

This is a guide and may change as the cut progresses. Same targets every day. Pick the row that matches your 7-day average bodyweight.

| Weight (7-day avg) | Calories (kcal) | Protein (g) | Fat (g) | Carbs (g) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 79.0 kg + | 2,100 | 175 | 65 | 205 |
| 78.0–78.9 kg | 2,075 | 173 | 65 | 200 |
| 77.0–77.9 kg | 2,050 | 171 | 65 | 195 |
| 76.0–76.9 kg | 2,025 | 168 | 65 | 192 |
| 75.0–75.9 kg | 2,000 | 166 | 65 | 187 |
| 74.0–74.9 kg | 1,975 | 164 | 65 | 184 |
| 73.0–73.9 kg | 1,950 | 162 | 65 | 178 |

### Rules

1. Weigh every morning after the toilet, before eating. Use the 7-day average to choose your row.
2. Protein = 1 g per lb of bodyweight. Hit it first every day.
3. From Week 3: losing under 0.25 kg/week → remove 150 kcal from carbs.
4. From Week 3: losing over 0.75 kg/week and lifts dropping → add 150 kcal to carbs.
5. Bad day: hit protein, stay roughly on calories.
